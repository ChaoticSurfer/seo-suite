# Backlink exchange template: guest post and link insertion

`seo-links` reads this file in part C when it writes outreach to blogs and media sites that publish guest posts or
add links to existing articles. The owner of a client site supplied the wording (2026-11-02); keep its structure and
fill every placeholder from the prospect's own page and the client's real facts.

## Rules for this template

- It asks the site for its terms, so many replies quote a price. A paid guest post or link insertion must carry
  `rel="sponsored"` (or `nofollow`): ask for it in the reply. A paid followed link is link spam (google-rules §4) and
  can bring a manual action. Free placements need no marking.
- Never send it bare. `{personal_line}` is one sentence about the prospect's page, written after reading it, and
  `{topics}` are 3–4 subjects the client knows first-hand and the prospect's readers want.
- Partner businesses (hotels, suppliers, associations) get a partnership email, not this one: a link-insertion ask
  doesn't fit a real business relationship.
- Write other languages natively for the prospect's market (seo-international), not word-for-word from English.
- Each message is an approval-batch item. One follow-up after 7–10 days, only if there's been no reply.

Placeholders: `{name}`, `{site}`, `{page_title}`, `{personal_line}`, `{brand}`, `{brand_url}`, `{what_we_do}`,
`{topics}`, `{sender_name}`, `{sender_email}`.

## EN

> Subject: Guest post / collaboration: {brand} × {site}
>
> Hi {name},
>
> I hope you are doing well!
>
> I'm reaching out on behalf of {brand} ({brand_url}), {what_we_do}, to explore a possible backlink collaboration.
>
> {personal_line}
>
> We'd like to contribute a guest post to your website, and to discuss adding a link to us in your existing content
> where it genuinely helps your readers (for example, on your "{page_title}" page). Topics we know first-hand:
> {topics}.
>
> We're happy to follow your editorial guidelines and preferred topics.
>
> Please let us know if this is something you would consider, and your terms and next steps.
>
> Thank you very much for your time, and we look forward to your reply.
>
> Best regards,
> {sender_name}, {brand} · {brand_url} · {sender_email}
